# Anudip
Information Technology & Business Systems
A comprehensive educational presentation covering IT fundamentals, business systems, data management, and data-driven decision-making.

📋 Overview
This presentation provides an in-depth exploration of Information Technology and Business Systems, designed for students and professionals seeking to understand the core concepts that drive modern organizations. It bridges the gap between business needs and technical solutions, featuring practical case studies and real-world applications.
